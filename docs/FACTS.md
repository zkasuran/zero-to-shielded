# Facts register

This file is the fact register for Zero to Shielded: every Zcash and Zodl claim a script may make, checked against a primary source on 2026-10-04. Scripts use VERIFIED rows only, in the words under "Script-safe wording". UNVERIFIED and CONTRADICTED rows stay out of scripts.

- Quotes are verbatim and 25 words or fewer. " / " separates two quotes. "..." marks a cut.
- App labels come from the official source strings, pinned to commits: Android `zodl-inc/zodl-android` at `9f4e719` (`ui-design-lib/src/main/res/ui/common/values/strings.xml`), iOS `zodl-inc/zodl-ios` at `6a7dc6b` (`secant/Resources/Localizable.xcstrings`), Android SDK `zodl-inc/zodl-android-wallet-sdk` at `ae538fc`. The old repo names `zashi-android` and `zashi-ios` redirect to these. "Android L123" and "iOS L123" in Notes are line numbers in those two strings files (for iOS, the line where the string's key starts).
- Android and iOS spell some labels differently. Captions must match the phone in the footage.
- Several support.zodl.com articles predate Ironwood. Their "Last updated" date is given where it matters.
